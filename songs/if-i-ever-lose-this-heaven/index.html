Getting to the next chord

Each change, the way it’s played in the arrangement (outlined) and other ways to play it. All of them were checked against the record’s vocal line; swap any of them in. “Hear it” plays the move on its own, slowly.

The trumpet

How to play itSoft and long, mostly straight tone, a slow swell into each long note and back out. The guitar carries the motion; you are the sustained voice above it, like an organ stop.
Where you restNot in verse 1, not in the first chorus. You come in more as the song goes on, and you finish it.
