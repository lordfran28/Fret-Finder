Prelude and Fugue in B♭ minor, BWV 867

Bach’s keyboard prelude and five-voice fugue on one guitar, a half step down in A minor so the open A and E strings can carry the bass. Each beat sits in one natural grip, mostly below the 12th fret: the top voice and the bass are kept, and the inner notes left out are listed. One diagram per beat in the prelude and per half bar in the fugue, in playing order from the first bar to the last.

  • KeyA minor (Bach’s B♭ minor)
  • Tuningstandard, no capo
  • Prelude24 bars, about 31 beats a minute
  • Fugue75 bars, about 103 beats a minute
  • Highest fret15th in the prelude, 19th in the fugue
